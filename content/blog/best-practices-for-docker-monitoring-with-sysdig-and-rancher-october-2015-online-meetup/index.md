Meetup Screenshot: Bill Maxwell Demonstrates Sysdig monitoring his
Rancher environment Yesterday we hosted an online meetup
with the team from [Sysdig](http://www.sysdig.com), in which
we discussed best practices for Docker monitoring, and some of
the unique challenges around applying monitoring policies to
containers. Over the course of the meetup, we introduced Rancher and
Sysdig, and demonstrated how we’re using Sysdig here at Rancher to
manage our containers. The meetup included a number of presentations,
and we’ve included the agenda below along with direct links to that
portion of the meetup if you’d like to jump ahead at all. • Rancher
Overview - Shannon Williams, Rancher ([direct
link](https://youtu.be/Efo-FGjBtbk?t=7m20s)) • Sysdig Overview - Chris
Crane, Sysdig ([direct link](https://youtu.be/Efo-FGjBtbk?t=11m22s)) •
Demo: Best practices for monitoring containers - BIll Maxwell, Rancher
([direct link](https://youtu.be/Efo-FGjBtbk?t=24m28s)) • Demo: Deep
container monitoring with Sysdig- Gianluca Borello, Sysdig ([direct
link](https://youtu.be/Efo-FGjBtbk?t=1h3m46s)) • Demo: Sysdig Cloud
([direct link](https://youtu.be/Efo-FGjBtbk?t=1h23m34s)) • Monthly
Update on latest Rancher enhancements ([direct
link](https://youtu.be/Efo-FGjBtbk?t=1h43m17s)) We’ve included the
entire video below. If you would like to try out Rancher, please [join
our beta program](https://info.rancher.com/beta). You can learn more
about Sysdig on their [website](http://www.sysdig.com). You can register
for our upcoming webinar on managing microservice upgrades with Docker
by following the link below.
