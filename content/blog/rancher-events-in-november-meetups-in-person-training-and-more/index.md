We’ve been really fortunate at Rancher to have an enthusiastic community of
users around the world, and we always looking forward to seeing and meeting our users in person. Here’s a few places Rancher will be in November. Please come say hi (and by the way, you can always keep track of where we’re headed at
[rancher.com/events](https://rancher.com/events))! Don’t see anything in
your area?

**[Let us know where we can meet you.](https://info.rancher.com/meetup-requests)**

### Meetups and Talks

You can see us around the world this month, as we discuss all the
challenges of managing containers in the enterprise:

#### Americas

**Nov 3** [Cloud Expo Santa
Clara](http://www.cloudcomputingexpo.com/event/session/3308) (SF Bay
Area) Rancher CEO Sheng Liang will keynote, discussing how containers
are influencing the future of cloud computing

**Nov 3** [ContainerDays
NYC](http://dynamicinfradays.org/events/2016-nyc/programme.html#realworld) (New
York, NY) Co-founder Shannon Williams and Rancher Automation Engineer
Nathan Valentine cover the real-life considerations and trade-offs
associated with using containers in production

**Nov 8** [KubeCon
2016](http://events.linuxfoundation.org/events/kubecon/program/schedule) (Seattle, WA) Principal engineer Alena Prokharchyk and Brian Scott of the Walt
Disney Company discuss standardizing Kubernetes deployments across
diverse infrastructure

**Nov 14** [Meetup: Kubernetes
Toronto](https://www.meetup.com/Kubernetes-Toronto/events/234838372/) (Canada)
with [CloudOps](https://www.cloudops.com/) Chris Sexsmith of Rancher
Labs provides insight on moving your application into Kubernetes
and into production...

**Nov 15** [Meetup: Kubernetes
Ottawa](https://www.meetup.com/Kubernetes-Ottawa/events/234861755/) (Canada)
with [CloudOps](https://www.cloudops.com/) ...then we’re bringing
the Kubernetes party to Ottawa

**Nov 16** [Meetup: Kubernetes
NYC](http://www.meetup.com/Kubernetes-Cloud-Native-New-York/events/234926720/?rv=ea1&_af=event&_af_eid=234926720&https=off) (New
York, NY) Shannon Williams shares how users are using the Rancher
container management platform to build their own private container
service

**Nov 18** [DevOps Days
Brasilia](https://www.devopsdays.org/events/2016-brasilia/welcome/) (Brasilia,
BR) with [BRCloud](http://www.brcloudservices.com/) We’re pleased
to cosponsor this event, which includes tutorials and workshops on using
Rancher and Docker containers in production!

#### Europe

**Nov 3** [DevOps
Manchester](https://www.meetup.com/DevOps-Manchester/events/234998938/)
UK Technical Lead Chris Urwin of Rancher Labs will discuss the adoption
of a container strategy, where to start, the gotchas to avoid, and the
overall benefits it can bring.

**Nov 17** [Connect
1.4](http://www.amsourcetechnology.com/14-devops-culture-containers) with [Infinity Works](https://www.infinityworks.com/) (Leeds, UK) Chris Urwin covers how DevOps, Docker containers, and the enterprise all come together in
the enterprise

#### APAC

**Nov 4** [DevOps Days India](http://devopsdaysindia.org/) with
\*\*[infraCloud.io](http://infracloud.io/) (Bangalore) Vishal Biyani, of
[Rancher Partner Network](https://rancher.com/partners-index/) company
infraCloud will present his talk, “Making Kubernetes Simple with
Rancher.”

**Nov 18** [MesosCon Asia](http://events.linuxfoundation.org/events/mesoscon-asia) (Hangzhou)
We’re sponsoring the greatest gathering of Mesos technologists in Asia,
and can’t wait to meet you there!

### In-person Training

Want to learn more about Rancher and containers than we can cover in our
[online monthly training](https://attendee.gotowebinar.com/rt/2905734731496917249)?

We’re hosting hands-on, in-person, in-depth training sessions that
include everything you need to know about using Rancher and containers
in production.

Our first in-person training will be **Nov 15-16** in Geneva, Switzerland.

We’ll also be holding another session later this year in Phoenix, Arizona. [Sign up here](https://rancher.com/training) to learn more and claim
your spot!

Can’t make the listed events, but still want to see
us? There’s always our online meetups (at
[rancher.com/meetup](https://rancher.com/online-meetups)). Or, [you can let us
know where we should go next](https://info.rancher.com/meetup-requests)!

**Giving your own talk on using Rancher**? DM
us [@Rancher\_Labs](http://twitter.com/rancher_labs), and let us know
how we can support you!
