The last twelve months have seen the Kubernetes landscape change dramatically. With much consolidation occurring among leading players, it’s never been more important to give customers a credible open source alternative.

To discuss the current market landscape, Rancher Labs Co-Founder and CEO Sheng Liang recently caught up with Swapnil Bhartiya, editor of technology news site [TFiR](https://www.tfir.io/) (aka The Fourth Industrial Revolution). In this episode of TFiR’s regular interview series, Let’s Talk Online, Sheng talks about the consolidation happening in the Kubernetes space and what it means for Rancher.
