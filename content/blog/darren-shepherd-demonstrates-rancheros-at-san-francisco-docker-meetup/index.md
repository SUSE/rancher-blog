Thanks to Docker, Orange and Blumberg Capital for hosting a great meetup

last night in San Francisco. Darren Shepherd, Chief Architect of Rancher

Labs introduced RancherOS for the first time, and answered questions

from the audience. Learn more about

[RancherOS](https://rancher.com/rancher-os/ "RancherOS"), or download it

from [GitHub](https://github.com/rancherio/os "RancherOS GitHub"). If

you’d like to learn more, Darren will be presenting RancherOS at an

online meetup on March 31st, 2015.
