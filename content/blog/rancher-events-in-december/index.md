[![rancher-logo_stacked-full-color](images/rancher_blog_rancher-logo_stacked-full-color.jpg)](images/rancher_blog_rancher-logo_stacked-full-color.jpg)We’re
winding down for the year, but you’ll still be able to check out
[Rancher](https://rancher.com/rancher)in a few places, live and in
real-time. We always look forward to meeting users whenever we can, and
hearing from you only helps make us better.

#### Meetups and Talks

**Dec 3, Montreal, CN: [Architecting Distributed Applications Across
Datacenters and
Clouds](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/kubernetes-mesos-or-swarm-architecting-distributed-applications-tickets-29760762241)**.
Join us as we discuss popular orchestrators, and strategies for
operationalizing distributed applications across diverse infrastructure.

**Dec 8, Tokyo, JPN**: **[Rancher Meetup
#2](https://connpass.com/event/44092/).** Our second meetup in Tokyo
showcases real-life use cases from local users, and a quick overview of
the latest features from Rancher 1.2.

**Dec 8, Online**: **[Rancher Training](https://rancher.com/training)**.
A hands-on overview of how to setup a Rancher deployment (you can always
check this out at [rancher.com/training](https://rancher.com/training)).

**Dec 14, Online** **Meetup: [Deep Dive on Rancher
1.2](https://rancher.com/online-meetups)**This month, we shipped the latest
version of Rancher, which brings in a massive set of improvements. See
the newest support for container storage and networking, and how we wrap
them into templates for container-ready environments.

**Dec 20**, **Lyon, FR**: **[Introduction to
Rancher](https://www.meetup.com/Docker-Lyon/events/234496858/)**. Rachid
Zarouali will be providing an introduction to the Rancher container
management platform at the Lyon Docker meetup, along with a short
overview of adjacent technologies like Prometheus and Grafana.

**Don’t see anything in your area?** [Let us know where we can meet
you](https://info.rancher.com/meetup-requests). **Are you talking about
Rancher in your town?** Let us know
[@Rancher\_Labs](http://twitter.com/rancher_labs), and we’ll give you a
shout out!
