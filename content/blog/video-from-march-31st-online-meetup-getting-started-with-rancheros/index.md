Yesterday we hosted our first Rancher online meetup, which was focused
on how to get started with RancherOS. For those of you who weren’t able
to attend our first online meetup on March 31st, we’ve posted a
recording. The meetup ran for more than two hours, and included demos
of RancherOS and Rancher, as well as dozens of questions about current
capabilities and some of the features we’re still working on. Our next
meetup is scheduled for April 29th, and will focus on running
microservices with Docker and Rancher. We’d love to see you there, you
can register by clicking the button below.
